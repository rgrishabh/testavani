How we work

From requirement to report

A clear, consultative process — so you know what will be tested, against which standard, and what you will receive.

  1. 01

    Share your requirement

    Tell us about the material or product and the purpose of testing. We help you select the tests and applicable standard, and send a quote within 1 hour.

  2. 02

    Submit your sample

    Drop off or courier your material sample to our lab in Greater Noida. We handle all types of metals, polymers and composites.

  3. 03

    Expert analysis

    Our certified scientists run comprehensive tests using ISO 17025 accredited methods and state-of-the-art instruments.

  4. 04

    Detailed report

    Receive a comprehensive, traceable test report with findings, interpretations and compliance status.

  5. 05

    Expert consultation

    Our metallurgists and polymer specialists are available to discuss findings and recommend solutions.

Applications

What our testing supports

  1. Product development

    Engineering and testing support during new product development — evaluating materials and prototypes against your targets.

  2. Quality control

    Routine testing of incoming raw materials, production batches and finished products.

  3. Material characterisation

    Identifying a material and measuring its chemical, physical and mechanical properties.

  4. Failure investigation

    Root-cause analysis of failed parts using composition, mechanical and microstructural examination.

  5. Compliance-oriented testing

    Testing against the applicable IS/BIS, ASTM, ISO or customer-specified requirements.

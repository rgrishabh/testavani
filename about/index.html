About AvaniEcocare Labs

Your trusted partner in precision material testing and scientific analysis

Avani Ecocare Labs Pvt. Ltd. is a testing and technical services organisation providing chemical, physical and mechanical testing solutions for manufacturers, suppliers, OEMs and industries.

Our Story

A dedicated laboratory for materials testing

Welcome to AvaniEcocare Labs, a dedicated testing laboratory specialising in polymer and metal analysis. We provide comprehensive, science-backed testing to support product development, quality assurance, failure analysis and compliance across a wide range of industries.

We perform chemical analysis, mechanical testing and metallurgical examination of alloys, plastics, rubber, coatings and other engineering materials to accurately define material composition and performance. Our testing capabilities also cover plywood and wood-based products, tiles and building materials, and utensils.

Our lab is powered by a team of highly qualified experts, including specialists with Master’s degrees in Plastic Technology and Metallurgy, bringing deep domain knowledge and a research-oriented approach to every project. All tests and inspections are performed by ISO 17025, NABL accredited and BIS approved laboratories.

Whether you’re developing a new product, troubleshooting material issues or validating the quality of raw materials, AvaniEcocare Labs is your trusted partner in polymer and metal testing.

How we work

From requirement to report

A clear, consultative process — so you know what will be tested, against which standard, and what you will receive.

  1. 01

    Share your requirement

    Tell us about the material or product and the purpose of testing. We help you select the tests and applicable standard, and send a quote within 1 hour.

  2. 02
